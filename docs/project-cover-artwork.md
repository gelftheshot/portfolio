## Generation prompts

### seasure

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: landscape portfolio project cover, wide 16:9 composition.
Create a polished editorial 3D concept visual representing "SeaSure", a marine AI platform for boat-specific troubleshooting, maintenance records and connection to marine service professionals.
Scene: a beautifully detailed miniature white express-cruiser motorboat viewed at a gentle elevated three-quarter angle, hovering just above a subtly rippled graphite water plane. Place the boat toward center-right. Add two restrained, elegantly layered translucent off-white data panels beside the boat: one with a fine-line engine systems diagram and the exact label "Vessel intelligence", one with a minimal service-history timeline and the exact label "Service history". Clean diagram graphics, not dense fake interface text. Fine technical connector lines link vessel engine, electrical and navigation areas to the panels.
Composition: refined cover-art photograph of a premium architectural model, generous negative space, large crisp white "SeaSure" wordmark in the upper left, small "MARINE INTELLIGENCE" below it. Keep all artwork within generous safe margins. Boat remains the primary visual; no oversize devices.
Palette: exclusively black, graphite, silver, gray and white. Dark charcoal backdrop, soft directional studio light, realistic subtle material texture, calm precise engineering aesthetic.
Constraints: product concept illustration, not a claimed screenshot of the live software. Do not imitate an existing company logo. No purple, pink, neon, colored glow, cartoon robots, brains, people, gradients, extra slogans, fabricated metrics, watermarks, or clutter. Make the boat realistic and the typographic hierarchy exceptionally clean.

Final asset: public/projects/seasure-cover.png

### projuno

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: landscape portfolio project cover, wide 16:9 composition, coordinated with an understated black-and-white software engineer portfolio.
Create a polished editorial 3D concept visual representing "Projuno", an AI platform for commercial construction and fit-out teams that connects project information, financial tracking, change traceability, compliance and workflow automation.
Scene: a meticulously crafted architectural cutaway scale model of a contemporary commercial office fit-out, viewed at a gentle elevated three-quarter angle, with clearly delineated rooms, small structural columns, clean partitions and a subtle floor-plan blueprint under it. Place model center-right on an off-white studio surface. Layer two elegant thin white business-data panels partially behind and beside the model: one sparse horizontal workflow labeled exactly "Work orders", "Variations", "Invoices"; one minimal monochrome line chart with exact heading "Project overview". Fine technical lines connect physical construction and project records.
Composition: large crisp black "Projuno" wordmark upper left, small "CONSTRUCTION INTELLIGENCE" below it, architectural editorial cover art with generous whitespace and carefully balanced geometry. All artwork safely inside frame; no laptop or phone.
Palette: exclusively white, light gray, silver and charcoal. Off-white background, natural shadows, tactile paper and concrete, soft directional daylight, highly refined material detail.
Constraints: conceptual illustration, not a claimed screenshot or reproduction of the live app UI. No invented logo, orange, purple, pink, colored glow, neon, generic AI brains, robots, people, gradients, fabricated metrics, extra slogans, watermarks or clutter. Crisp restrained typography and believable model craftsmanship.

Final asset: public/projects/projuno-cover.png
